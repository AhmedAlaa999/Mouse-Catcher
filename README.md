# MouseCatcher

A simple 2D platformer game where your mission is to explore the level, avoid obstacles, and find the **special Blue Cheese** to win!

## Gameplay

Your objective is simple:
- Explore the map and navigate through the level.
- Avoid falling into dangerous areas.
- Find and collect the special Blue Cheese.
- Collect the Blue Cheese to complete the game and win!

Be careful! Falling into the Death Zone means you lose.

## Features

- 2D platformer gameplay.
- Player movement and animations.
- Floating Blue Cheese collectible.
- Collection sound effect.
- Win screen when collecting the Blue Cheese.
- Lose screen when falling into the Death Zone.
- Retry and Main Menu options.

## How to Win

Find the Blue Cheese and touch it to trigger the victory screen.

## How to Lose

Fall into the Death Zone to trigger the lose screen.

## Controls

- **A / Left Arrow:** Move left.
- **D / Right Arrow:** Move right.
- **Space:** Jump.
